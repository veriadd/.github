## What is Veriadd?
Veriadd is address KYC/KYB infrastructure for Nigeria. We help businesses verify customer and business addresses through a single API call, supporting onboarding and compliance workflows with reliable address verification.

## Why it matters
For fintechs, lenders, and regulated businesses in Nigeria, address verification is a critical part of risk, onboarding, and compliance. Manual checks are slow and inconsistent, while poor address data increases fraud and operational risk. Veriadd provides a trusted verification layer built for the Nigerian context.

## What we're building
- Address verification for Nigeria as a SaaS platform.
- A single API-call experience to verify Nigerian addresses.
- Infrastructure for fintechs, lenders, and businesses that need address checks for onboarding and compliance.
